Describe the problem statement

KAIRÓS · 01

Enterprises have solved metric detection and left metric explanation unautomated. A dashboard says North revenue fell 18.8%. It cannot say the fall is three enterprise accounts that stopped reordering after a warehouse missed its dispatch SLA ten days earlier — because that sentence lives in service tickets, call transcripts and a dispatch log no BI tool reads.

Three structural failures keep the translation manual. Noise blindness: anomaly tools alert on deviation, not consequence. Correlation theatre: “insight” features surface what moved alongside a metric, and fluent prose makes a weak correlation read as settled. Organisational amnesia: the company solved this pattern fourteen months ago and that memory sits in a dead slide.

The measurable consequence is decision latency, not data latency. Pipelines refresh hourly; the explanation refreshes weekly. And the binding obstacle is not modelling — across twenty organisations, data quality was the only barrier every respondent named.

Proposed solution

KAIRÓS · 02

KAIRÓS — the opportune moment — is an explanation layer that sits on top of the BI stack a client already owns. It detects a material movement, decomposes it exactly, grades every explanation against a standard of proof, and recommends an action with a named owner. Or it refuses, and prices the cheapest experiment that would settle the question.

The architecture rests on one commitment: language models are strong at proposing causal explanations and near-random at inferring causation from correlation. So the model proposes and phrases — it never decides and never computes. That is enforced at runtime by a closed evidence packet and a numeric guard that rejects any figure absent from it.

Nothing below the second rung of the evidence ladder is ever phrased as a cause. When two explanations tie, KAIRÓS abstains and prices a separating test rather than guessing.

Three enforcement points, all of them before the model sees anything
01
Pre-flight refusal
A persona whose row entitlement does not intersect the request is refused before the query runs. We never execute and return empty — “no data” and “not allowed” are different answers.

Row filters become WHERE clauses, so restricted rows never enter a dataframe in the first place.

Denied measures are stripped from the evidence packet itself. A test asserts the Supply Chain packet contains no rupee value anywhere in its JSON.

Phased roadmap

KAIRÓS · 09
Scope expands only after analyst-graded precision clears the bar
PHASE 1 · 0–3 MONTHS
Prove precision
One P&L metric, one function. Semantic, fitness, sift and split on the existing layer. Narratives reviewed, never auto-published.
Exit — analyst-graded precision clears the agreed bar
PHASE 2 · 3–6 MONTHS
Add evidence and memory
Connect unstructured sources with entitlements and redaction. Seed playbooks from past incidents. Enable the ladder and abstention.
Exit — ≥60% of material movements reach L2 or better
PHASE 3 · 6–12 MONTHS
Close the loop
Multi-KPI, multi-persona, outcome logging, the ranker trained on the client’s resolved episodes. Recommendations stay proposed.
Exit — recommendation calibration measurable
PHASE 4 · 12 MONTHS +
Productise
Industry-tuned causal graph and playbook packs, packaged as a delivery accelerator rather than a one-off build.
Exit — reuse across engagements
Precision is the only gate. Nothing about phase 2 begins until an analyst blind-grading diagnoses says the engine is right often enough to be worth reading. That ordering is what separates this from a pilot that ships insight volume and is quietly muted six weeks later.

Risk and mitigation

KAIRÓS · 10
Each mitigation is built into the engine, not bolted on afterwards
RiskMitigation
Confident but wrongL2 evidence floor; an explicit Unknown state; mandatory citation; the numeric guard rejects unverifiable figures
Spurious causesThe curated causal graph blocks inadmissible mechanisms; the placebo must validate before the top rung is awarded
Poor data qualityA fitness gate runs first across five dimensions and may halt the run; class-level completeness catches a load that drops one kind of row while totals hold
The ground movesDrift monitored separately for data and model; a ranker scoring outside its training support has its authority withdrawn for that run
Definitional disputesCompeting KPI definitions reconciled once, before analysis, with the resolution, the authority and the gap recorded
Over-trustRival hypotheses shown by default; actions stay proposed; full machine delegation reserved and never assigned
Sensitive evidenceRow, column and domain entitlements applied before prompt assembly; PII redacted pre-retrieval; withheld items surfaced as a count
RegulatoryArt. 12 post-hoc reconstruction satisfied by full lineage; Art. 14 oversight by never auto-executing; India’s DPDP makes purpose limitation on call data a design constraint
Platform commoditisationWe deliberately do not rebuild text-to-SQL or the semantic layer — both are free platform features now. The moat is the standard of proof, the evidence outside the warehouse, and the memory
Stated openly
Effect sizes are recovered because they were planted. On real data expect more L1 and L2 and fewer L3s — a counterfactual needs a genuinely untreated cohort, and often none exists.
And the honest limit
Forecasting is trend continuation only. Delivery writes to an outbox and does not send. Confidence is composed, not yet calibrated against realised outcomes.
